# Scheduling Algorithms Comparison

| Feature | FCFS | SJF | Priority | Round Robin |
|----------|------|------|----------|------------|
| Basis | Arrival | Burst Time | Priority | Time Quantum |
| Starvation | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Fairness | Low | Low | Medium | High |
| Response Time | Poor | Good | Good | Excellent |
| Time Sharing | No | No | Limited | Yes |

## Conclusion

FCFS → Simple

SJF → Minimum Waiting Time

Priority → Important Tasks First

Round Robin → Fair CPU Sharing
