# Day 7 Notes

## Priority Scheduling

Priority Scheduling executes processes based on priority.

Lower priority number generally indicates higher priority.

---

## Types

1. Non-Preemptive Priority Scheduling
2. Preemptive Priority Scheduling

---

## Advantages

- Important tasks execute first.
- Suitable for real-time applications.

---

## Disadvantages

### Starvation

Low-priority processes may wait indefinitely.

---

## Aging

A technique used to prevent starvation by gradually increasing the priority of waiting processes.

---
