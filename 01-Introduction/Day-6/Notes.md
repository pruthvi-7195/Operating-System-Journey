# Day 6 Notes

## Shortest Job First (SJF)

Shortest Job First executes the process with the smallest burst time first.

---

## Advantages

- Minimum Average Waiting Time
- Efficient CPU Utilization
- Better Performance

---

## Disadvantages

### Starvation

Long processes may wait indefinitely if shorter jobs continue to arrive.

---

## Types of SJF

1. Non-Preemptive SJF
2. Preemptive SJF (Shortest Remaining Time First)

---
